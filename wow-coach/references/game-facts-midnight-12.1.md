# Verified game facts — Midnight 12.1, Season 2

Game-mechanic facts verified during coaching sessions for WoW **Midnight 12.1, Season 2** (Aug 2026). These correct stale assumptions; each was either confirmed in-game by the player or settled from their own logs.

- **Enchant slots: back and wrist have NO enchants in Midnight** (player-confirmed in-game, 2026-08-26). Head, shoulders, chest, legs, feet, rings, weapon DO (all seen with enchant_ids in the player's exports). Never flag back/wrist as missing enchants.
- **Catalyst (S2): currency is "Venomblight Manaflux", CurrencyID 3465, cap 1/8, accrues every two weeks** (tooltip screenshot). Also earnable from M+/raid/delves/rated PvP after the Catalyst Unbound feat. The SimC export's other catalyst_currencies entries (2813/3269/3116/3378) are OLD seasons — do not count them as charges.
- **Catalyst output INHERITS the base item's secondary stats** (tooltip: "will inherit the secondary stats of the original equipment"). Old-season tier pieces are ineligible (transmog-only conversion); non-tier-slot conversion grants no set bonus. → A charge is only worth spending on a good-stat base, and only as the piece that completes a bonus.
- **Summon Demonic Tyrant: 60s cooldown observed** (observed min recast intervals in the player's logs, 2026-08-26) — settles the 60-vs-90s guide conflict for that build.
- **Grimoire: Felguard no longer exists in 12.1** — replaced by Grimoire: Imp Lord / Fel Ravager (2-min CD, grants an extra Spell Lock/Singe Magic use). Never grade or query the old spell.
- **Demo tier set = "Damned Necrolyte's Shattered Restraints" (set 2066)**: 2pc +10% Wild Imp / +20% Implosion; 4pc 20% imp self-implode at 350%/315% (buffed 2026-08-15). No published sim numbers for the bonuses as of Aug 2026.
- S2 M+ pool: Murder Row, Altar of Fangs, Den of Nalorakk, The Blinding Vale, Voidscar Arena, King's Rest, Temple of Sethraliss, Ruby Life Pools. Murder Row timer 34 min. Altar of Fangs par 30 min (+2 at 24, +3 at 18).
- **Fel Armor is BOTH a gain and a damage source, and a death recap will show it as both** (settled from a two-night log census by the wowdps dev session, 2026-09-06: 8436 `SPELL_ABSORBED` lines vs 2160 `SPELL_PERIODIC_DAMAGE`). The passive absorbs a hit, then deals the deferred remainder back to the warlock as self-sourced periodic ticks — the same shape as a Brewmaster's stagger. So small self-sourced Fel Armor damage rows in a recap are its ticks and are legitimate; the absorb rows are gains. **Only the tick can ever be a killing blow.** Do not read a Fel Armor row as a classification bug.
- **A death is only "unavoidable" relative to the player's MAX health, which the recap gives you** (`health_after.max`). 2026-09-06: a 552,990 melee read as a one-shot until checked against an 808,122 pool — it was 68% of max, i.e. survivable above ~69%, and killed the player only because they had been parked at 22% for several ticks. Always divide before grading a big hit; the rubric's "≥150% max HP = unavoidable" test is meaningless without it.

**Why:** these facts invalidated four coaching findings in one night (wrist/back enchants, "8 banked charges", catalyze-the-helm, Grimoire counts); re-deriving them from guides gets them wrong.
**How to apply:** treat as ground truth for 12.1 sessions; these outrank guide prose AND research-agent output when they conflict. Re-verify on patch change, and append new player-confirmed facts here (dated) rather than in session notes. Character-specific state stays in memory (tranq-performance-coaching).
- **Kings' Rest (S2 M+): the "Minion of Zul" mobs near the entrance FIXATE and FEAR anyone who walks too close** (player-reported 2026-09-11; log shows `Fixate` from Minion of Zul applied to the warlock at 22 s and 47 s into two consecutive +12 starts, the second attempt was abandoned at 85 s with zero player deaths). A key that dies in the first 90 s of Kings' Rest with no deaths is this pull, not a throughput problem — check `SPELL_AURA_APPLIED … "Minion of Zul","Fixate"` before grading anything.
- **The S2 damage potion logs as "Light's Potential", not "…Potion"** (verified 2026-09-12, Tranqster Voidscar +12): a raw-log grep for `potion` finds only health potions and reports "no potion" on a pull that had one. Use the `breakdown` timeline's `consumable` marks (label "Light's Potential", `caster` = the player's GUID) or grep that exact name. Trash potions ARE legitimate in keys when the group lusts on trash — check the lust timestamp before calling a trash potion wasted.
- **bloodmallet publishes DEVOURER demon hunter sims as of its 2026-09-09 run** (SimC ba72a9d, tier MID2) — the earlier "no Devourer data" note is obsolete. `scripts/fetch-trinket-sims.sh` / `fetch-secondary-sims.sh` now include `demon_hunter/devourer`; the local TSVs were refreshed 2026-09-15. Still never substitute Vengeance rows.
- **Voidcore bonus rolls on HEROIC bosses can award MYTHIC-track loot (1/6)** (player-verified 2026-09-23: Heroic Sszorak Voidcore → Venomfang Cleaver 321, Mythic 1/6). A Voidcore drop is therefore judged on its **upgrade ceiling**, not the ilvl it lands at — never dismiss one for being below a crafted piece's current ilvl.
- **Health potions and combat potions do NOT share a cooldown in 12.1** (settled from the log 2026-09-25, Tränq Heroic Lost Explorers `00ff98a06b78611c-1790368258367`: Silvermoon Health Potion at 310 s, Light's Potential at 315 s). Never frame a health potion as having "cost" the damage potion.
- **Upgrade tracks and crests, S2 (DB-verified 2026-09-30, wago.tools DB2 + wowhead tooltips, build 69933 — not in-game):** crests are "Mistcrests", currency ids in the SimC `upgrade_currencies=` line: **3442 Adventurer (→282) · 3443 Veteran (→295) · 3444 Champion (→308) · 3445 Hero (→321) · 3446 Myth (→334)**. Track/step bonus ids seen on gear: 12830 Veteran 6/6 295 · **12838 Champion 6/6 308 (MAXED)** · 12842 Hero 2/6 308 · 12843 Hero 3/6 311 · 12846 Hero 6/6 321 · 12849 Myth 1/6 318 · 12850 Myth 2/6 321 · 12854 Myth 6/6 334. Two items at the same ilvl can sit on different tracks — a Champion 6/6 308 is capped while a Hero 2/6 308 copy of the same tier piece goes to 321; read the track bonus id before calling a slot "upgradable" or "maxed". Per-step crest cost not verified.
- **Enchant ids are distinct enchants, not rank triples** (wago SpellItemEnchantment, 2026-09-30): ring 8025 Silvermoon's Alacrity / 7969 Zul'jin's Mastery; weapon 7981 Jan'alai's Precision / 7983 Berserker's Rage / 8041 Arcane Mastery; helm 7961 Empowered Hex of Leeching; shoulder 8031 Silvermoon's Mending; chest 7987 Mark of the Worldsoul; boots 7993 Shaladrassil's Roots. All carry the Tier2 quality icon. Gem 240892 = Flawless Masterful Peridot (rare). Two rings with different enchant ids is a stat choice, not a missing rank.
- **Living Venom (Altar of Fangs trash) — Venom Burst fires ON DEATH and hits the whole party** (log-settled 2026-10-01, Tranqlock Altar +15: one Implosion at 20:13:24 hit 6 Living Venoms, they died within 1.4 s, and each of the 6 bursts landed on all 5 players; the warlock died from 67 %). Never grade a Venom Burst death as positioning. AoE-bursting a Venom pack is the error; stagger the kills.
- **Dark Pact recasts as fast as 45 s and the Demonic Healthstone is reusable in combat** (log-settled 2026-10-01 across two nights: Dark Pact min recast 45 s = 60 s base − Frequent Donor; Demonic Healthstone min gap 70 s, sources give a 60 s CD, Pact of Gluttony). A death "64 s after the last Healthstone" may still have had one ready — check before grading it mitigable.
- **Voidscar Arena: an unkicked Shadowbolt Volley (Voidtouched Magi) hit every DPS for ~689k at +15** (log 2026-10-02, 75 % of a 914k pool). A death right after a volley that got through is a missed kick first, a defensive second.
- **Mythic Sszorak (enc 3420, diff 16) — log-settled 2026-10-26, Tranqlock's team, 40 pulls:** **Virulence chains between nearby players** (pull 32: debuff jumped through 16 players in 0.55 s, 15.9M damage, 11 took 2+ hits, one took 8; wiped pulls 2/28/29/32 with 14–16 dead each, always ~29 s or ~76 s in) → a raid-spread problem, not a personal one. **Tempest** goes out on ~6 players per wave; extra hits come from standing near another Tempest target (most players 1 hit ~280k; a stacked player took 3 = 837k in 1 s). **Mutilate** is split across two soak groups of ~9, followed by a Mutilated Gash bleed (~60k/s, ~83k/s late). **Viscous Cyst** was the #1 killer (109 deaths by pull 23). **Ula'tek's Presence** pulses ~50k raid-wide and ramps late (380k at 2:38). **A death at high HP with a self-tick (Fel Armor/Burning Rush) as killing_blow is a fall**: either a jump on the wipe call (player-confirmed: late in the pull, Burning Rush on, raid collapsing) or a Raging Crosswinds knock-off / misstep carrying a Cyst (early, raid still alive). Ask before grading one.
